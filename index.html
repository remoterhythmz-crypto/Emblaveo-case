ANTIBIOTIC EXPOSURE · SIMULATION DETAIL

Prolonged meropenem, cefepime, and vancomycin during the postoperative ICU stay. This exposure is a risk context for resistant opportunistic infection; it does not by itself prove causation.

SUSCEPTIBILITY & MECHANISM

Intrinsic resistance to many standard β-lactams and carbapenems is associated with L1 metallo-β-lactamase and L2 serine β-lactamase. In this simulated record, aztreonam-avibactam MIC: 4/4 µg/mL.

Arthur’s name, age, portrait, and some history details are fictional. The linked paper reports a different 59-year-old patient and a different prior-antibiotic history.
